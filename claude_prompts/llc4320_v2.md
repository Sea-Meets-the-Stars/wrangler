- **15 (iteration vs. date) — answered by Dimitris' own notes.** The loop quoted in `dimitris_notes_v2.txt` (and `ExtractFields.m` lines 13-28) is the date rule: *date = folder start time + n hours*, where n is the file's 0-based position in its folder's sorted listing.
